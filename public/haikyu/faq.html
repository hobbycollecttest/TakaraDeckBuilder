Haikyu!! VOBACA!! BREAK FAQ

Short answers to the questions new Haikyu!! VOBACA!! BREAK players ask the most. The answers follow Takara Tomy's official English Q&A sheets, put into our own words and grouped by topic.

New to the game? Start with our how to play guide.

This page is a summary by Hobby Collect, not an official document. The official Q&A sheets are on the official English rules page. If this page and the official answers ever disagree, the official answers win.

Building a deck

How many cards are in a deck?

Exactly 40.

How many copies of the same card can I use?

As many as you like. The only limit is on Event cards: you can use up to 8 Event cards in total.

Hand, Guts and Areas

Is there a hand size limit?

No. You can hold any number of cards.

How many Guts can an Area hold?

Any number.

What if I play a Character into an Area that already has one?

Put the new Character on top. The cards underneath become Guts.

Can I block with only a Side Blocker?

No. The first Character you play into the Block Area is always the Center Blocker.

Can a Side Blocker pay Guts from under the Center Blocker?

Yes. A Side Blocker pays Guts from the Block Area.

My Center Blocker and Side Blocker both have skills. Which goes first?

You choose the order. If you have enough Guts you can pay for both.

Parameters

Can I play a Character with a 0 into that Area?

Yes.

Can I play a Character with a dash into that Area?

No. A dash means the Character cannot be played into the Area for that parameter.

Can a parameter go below 0?

Yes. Keep counting it as a negative number, such as -1 or -2.

The rally

Can I skip a phase or go back to an earlier one?

No. The phases always happen in order.

Can I send the ball back with just a Receive or a Set?

No, unless a card skill lets you.

The Offense Point is 0 and my Defense Point is 0. Did I defend?

Yes. A tie is a successful block or receive.

What happens when a deck runs out?

Nothing special. Keep playing. When you would draw a card and cannot, you simply do not draw.

Event cards

How do I use an Event card?

Put it face up from your hand into your Event Area and use its skill.

Can I play more than one Event in the same phase?

Yes, any number, unless a card says otherwise.

Can I play an Event in a different phase from its icon?

No. An Event can only be played in the phase shown by its play timing icon.

Do the effects of several Events stack?

Yes. Bonuses and penalties from Character skills and Event skills add together.

Can I play an Event even if its condition is not met?

Yes, as long as the timing is right. It goes to the Event Area even if its skill does nothing. The exception is when something stops you from playing Event cards at all.

Can I play an Event first and then use my Character's [Enter] skill?

No. Finish the [Enter] skill before you play the Event. If the Event itself makes a Character enter, do that Character's [Enter] skill and then finish the Event.

Declaring Lost

What if I cannot play a Character?

You declare Lost. Your opponent takes the set and the game moves to the Interval.

Can I declare Lost on purpose?

Yes. Choosing not to play a Character means declaring Lost.

I declared Lost. What happens to "at the end of this turn" skills?

They do not happen. The effect just ends.

And skills that say "when you declare Lost"?

They happen right after you declare Lost, before the Interval.

What happens to a Side Blocker when I declare Lost?

It goes to the Drop Area.

I have 7 or more cards in hand at the Interval. Do I discard?

No. You keep them. If you lost the set you still add 1 Score card to your hand.

Skills

Can I do the parts of a skill in any order?

No. Follow the text from the start, in order.

One skill allows an action and another forbids it. Which wins?

The one that forbids it.

A skill says I may do something to use it. What if I cannot do it?

Then you cannot use the skill. And if you do it, you must carry out the rest of the skill.

Both players have a skill that triggers at the same time. Who goes first?

The player whose turn it is resolves all of theirs first, then the other player.

Does a "during this turn" effect end if the Character leaves the court?

No. Once used, it lasts for the stated time.

Does an [Enter] skill work if the Character entered through another skill?

Yes.

What is the difference between "2 cards" and "up to 2 cards"?

"2 cards" means exactly 2. "Up to 2 cards" means you choose 0, 1 or 2.

What does "original" mean?

The number or information printed on the card, before anything changes it.

What does "if all of your characters are Karasuno" mean?

Every Character on your court must be from that team. Only the top card in each Area counts as a Character. Guts underneath do not count. If you have no Characters, the condition is not met.

A skill stops me from adding cards to my hand. Can I still draw?

No. Drawing counts as adding a card to your hand.
