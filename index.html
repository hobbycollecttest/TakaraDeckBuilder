HORO-BEAT Deck Builder

Build, check and share decks for the HORO-BEAT trading card game by Takara Tomy. All 194 cards are here in English and in the original Japanese, with the official rules built in: exactly 50 cards and up to 4 copies of each card.

Search and filter cards by color, cost, type and tribe, see your cost curve, save decks to your account, browse public decks from other players, and download a picture of your deck to share.
