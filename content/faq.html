These are the official HORO-BEAT FAQ answers from Takara Tomy, translated into English. They cover the questions that come up the most: what order things happen in after a battle, how Ena colors work for Boosts and Overbeat, and rulings on specific cards like Yosuzume and Faceless Rider.

Unofficial English translation of the official Japanese FAQ on the Takara Tomy Horobeat site (17 entries). If this translation and the Japanese original ever disagree, the Japanese original takes precedence.

Rules

What order are effects and damage resolved in after a battle is decided?

They're always resolved in this order:

  1. Both players choose not to use any more Waza or Boosts, and the battle's result is decided.
  2. The winning Horobeat's "when this wins a battle" effects activate.
  3. The losing Horobeat's "when this loses a battle" effects activate. (Barrier counts as one of these. It still reduces the damage the player takes even if the Horobeat is destroyed afterward.)
  4. The losing Horobeat takes damage. If it was already downed, it is destroyed.
  5. The losing player takes damage.
  6. If any Hearts were destroyed, carry out the Check.
  7. "When the battle ends" effects activate.
  8. All Waza and Boosts used in this battle are put into the Graveyard.

Are there color restrictions on the Ena I pay when using a Boost?

Yes. To use a Boost, you must pay by making 1 Ena of the same color as that Horobeat used.

Are there color restrictions on the Ena I pay when playing a Horobeat with Overbeat?

Yes. The Horobeat's color must be included in either the Ena you pay or the Overbeat base.

What happens if an effect can't be fully carried out?

Do as much of it as you can. For example, if you're told to draw 3 cards but only have 2 left in your deck, draw those 2.

Several effects activated at the same time. What order are they resolved in?

The First Player's effects are resolved first. If the First Player has several unresolved effects, they choose one and resolve it.

Once none of the First Player's effects remain, the Second Player's effects are resolved the same way.

If resolving a Second Player's effect causes a new First Player effect to activate at the same timing, that new effect is resolved first.

A player took 3 or more damage at once and 2 or more Hearts were destroyed at the same time. How does the Check work?

When multiple Hearts are destroyed at once, they all move to the Check Zone together. You can look at the cards in your Check Zone, so their owner checks them.

The owner then chooses a card whose Trigger effect they want to activate, and activates it. Repeat this until the Check Zone is empty or there are no more Trigger effects you want to activate.

If you choose not to activate any more Trigger effects while cards are still in the Check Zone, those cards go to their owner's hand.

I played a Horobeat with an Ena Reading effect without meeting the condition. If the condition is met later, does the Ena Reading effect turn on?

Yes, it does. However, if the Ena Reading effect has a timing that has already passed, such as "when this Horobeat is played", it can't go back and activate.

I used a Waza with a Resonance effect without meeting the condition. If the condition is met later for some reason, does the Resonance effect turn on?

Yes, it does. However, if the Resonance effect only activates at a specific timing, such as "when this Waza is used", it may not get a chance to activate.

I used a Waza with a Resonance effect while meeting the condition. If the condition stops being met later for some reason, does the Resonance effect still work?

No, it stops working. If the Resonance effect activates at a specific timing, such as "at the end of the battle", the condition must be met at that point.

Common Effects

Can an Over Trigger or Last Over Trigger Overbeat onto a Horobeat that has "You cannot Overbeat onto this Horobeat."?

No. Effects can't Overbeat onto a Horobeat with "You cannot Overbeat onto this Horobeat." either.

A Horobeat has "While this Horobeat is in a line, its cost is 1 higher." Can I Overbeat it with a Horobeat whose cost is 1 higher than its original cost?

No. While it's in a line, its cost is 1 higher, so it has the same cost as the Horobeat you'd be playing. That means you can't Overbeat it.

When a card effect has me "look at" cards in a hidden zone, do I have to show them to my opponent?

No, you don't.

Some Waza can only be used when a specific condition is met. If a card effect makes me use one, do I still need to meet the condition?

Yes, you do. A Waza with an effect like "Can only be used when…" can't be used unless the condition is met, even when another card's effect uses it.

If an effect tries to use a Waza whose condition isn't met, the Waza isn't used and stays in the zone it was in.

Card-Specific

Yosuzume

Horobeat · Red · Cost 3 · Power 5500 · Boost: Yes · Fuga Family · Entry Trigger · R · Illustrator: rikose · HB-BP01 Booster Pack Vol. 1: Horobeat

●This Horobeat cannot be played unless there is a Horobeat with 4000 or more power in an adjacent line.

Q. My Horobeat in Line 1 has an original power below 4000, but with the Waza and Boosts in Line 1's Waza Zone added, its power is 4000 or more. At this point, one of my Hearts is destroyed and Yosuzume moves to my Check Zone. Can I use Yosuzume's Entry Trigger to play it into Line 2?

A. Yes, you can. During the Check, the power of Waza and Boosts in a Waza Zone is still added to your Horobeat in the same line.

For example, if you have a 2000-power Leomaru in Line 1 with a +2000 Boost, you can play Yosuzume into Line 2.

Faceless Rider

Horobeat · Red · Cost 4 · Power 7000 · Boost: Yes · Fuga Family · Double Damage · Over Trigger · R · Illustrator: ROBUJA · Great Battle Holomotion Pack, Vol. 1

●Double Damage (If this Horobeat wins a battle, deal 2 damage to the opposing player instead of 1.)
●If you play this Horobeat from your hand, it can't be played with Overbeat.

Q. Faceless Rider has "If you play this Horobeat from your hand, it can't be played with Overbeat." Can I still play it with Overbeat when its Over Trigger activates?

A. Yes, you can. When the Over Trigger activates, Faceless Rider is in the Check Zone, not your hand, so you can play it with Overbeat.

Mystic Fox Nanao

Horobeat · Red · Cost 4 · Power 5000 · Boost: Yes · Maboroshi Commune · Over Trigger · SR · Illustrator: YUMEKO · HB-BP01 Booster Pack Vol. 1: Horobeat

●When this Horobeat's battle ends, choose 1 of your red Ena. Make it unused, then recover this Horobeat.

Q. Mystic Fox Nanao's "when this Horobeat's battle ends" effect activates, but I don't have any red Ena. Does Mystic Fox Nanao still recover?

A. Yes, it does. Even if you can't make an Ena unused, the recover part of the effect still happens.

Yamanba Yamahime

Horobeat · Blue · Cost 3 · Power 4500 · Boost: No · Chara Order · Entry Trigger · UC · Illustrator: I☆LA · HB-BP01 Booster Pack Vol. 1: Horobeat

●When this Horobeat's battle ends, choose 1 cost-1 Waza that was used in this battle. You may use that card in the next line.
(Waza can't be used in a line with no Horobeat.)

Q. Can Yamanba Yamahime's effect choose a Waza my opponent used on the Horobeat directly opposite?

A. No, it can't. When effect or condition text doesn't say whose card or zone it means, it means your own card or zone.
